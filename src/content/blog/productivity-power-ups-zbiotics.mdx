## Good Times 😂

Drinking provides social enjoyment and relaxation. However, alcohol is toxic and excessive consumption creates hangovers that significantly impact productivity.

The question is: how can accomplished professionals balance drinking socially while minimizing next-day performance losses?

## Hangovers 😵

Hangovers stem from **acetaldehyde**—formed during alcohol metabolism—which is the culprit behind hangover symptoms. Acetaldehyde "binds to receptors in your brain, creates oxidative stress" causing nausea, vomiting, tremors, dizziness, and malaise.

The body's liver becomes overwhelmed processing acetaldehyde during heavy drinking.
